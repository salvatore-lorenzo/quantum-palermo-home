NOTES
- CMYK derived with the FOGRA39 profile (relative colorimetric, black-point compensation), rounded to 5%.
- Blue prints slightly duller than on screen (CMYK gamut limit). Proof before large runs.
- No Pantone equivalents are specified; match to a physical swatch book if needed.

TYPEFACES
Wordmark: initials q and P are custom-drawn; letters u, n, i, a are Audiowide Regular (Astigmatic, SIL OFL 1.1),
converted to outlines and scaled to a 42-unit x-height. The wordmark is supplied as outlines; never retype it.
Descriptor / companion text: IBM Plex Sans (Regular, Medium, SemiBold), SIL OFL 1.1, github.com/IBM/plex. Fallback: Arial / Helvetica.
Licences: source/fonts/OFL.txt (Plex), source/fonts/OFL-Audiowide.txt.

CLEAR SPACE: x on all sides, x = outer diameter of one emblem node (wordmark only: x-height of u n i a).
MINIMUM WIDTH (print / screen): horizontal 38 mm / 170 px; horizontal+descriptor 50 mm / 230 px; stacked 22 mm / 95 px;
wordmark 22 mm / 90 px; full emblem 12 mm / 48 px; compact emblem 6 mm / 24 px; monogram 4 mm / 16 px.

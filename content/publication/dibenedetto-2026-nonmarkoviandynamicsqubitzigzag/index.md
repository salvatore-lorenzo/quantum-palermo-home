abstract: We present an analytical study of the dynamics of a qubit coupled to the
  zigzag edge of a photonic honeycomb lattice. Leveraging a resolvent operator approach
  and exact lattice Green's functions, we map the spectrum of qubit-photon bound states
  and complex poles to uncover the long-time scaling laws of the qubit population.
  Unlike a qubit coupled to the bulk of graphene, the presence of a zero-energy flat
  band connecting the Dirac points suppresses long-time branch-cut-induced tails,
  instead driving damped Rabi oscillations. Potential experimental platforms are briefly
  discussed.
